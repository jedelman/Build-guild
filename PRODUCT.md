# Product

## Register

product

(App UI where design serves the task. The bar is earned familiarity — a builder
fluent in Linear / Stripe / Notion should trust it instantly — executed with a
crafted guild-hall character, not theatrics.)

## Users

Independent builders (engineers, designers, ML/data people, writers) who are
done job-hunting alone and want to team up and earn together. They arrive from a
Bluesky thread, already on atproto, comfortable with the idea that their identity
and work are theirs. Two sides:

- **Builders** publish a character sheet (class, peer-endorsed skill-peaks,
  projects, linked repos), form guilds, and claim paid quests as a party.
- **Patrons** post quests (work + a reward) and get matched to a suitably diverse
  party by the guild's combined skill coverage.

## Product Purpose

Build Guild is a **team job board** for builders. The loop: declare skills →
peers endorse them (consensus peaks) → form a guild → claim quests with bounties
as a party → split the reward. Skill strength is what your peers vouch for, not a
self-rating. Identity, skills, and endorsements are user-owned atproto records;
our D1 is an index. Success = builders find each other and get paid for real work
together.

## Brand Personality

A nocturnal guild hall rendered as a precise instrument. Confident, crafted,
unmistakably its own thing — gold as the signature of rank and value, arcane teal
as the interactive accent, hairline depth over decoration. Numbers (peaks, Guild
Power, bounties) read as data you can trust. Three words: **crafted, decisive,
trustworthy**.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-tool aesthetic: purple/cyan gradients, glassmorphism, neon-on-black,
  gradient text, hero-metric templates.
- Identical card grids repeated across every view (the roster, guilds, and quests
  must each have their own rhythm).
- Emoji as iconography (use the crafted inline-SVG set).
- Muted gray text that fails contrast "for elegance."
- SaaS-job-board sameness (Indeed/LinkedIn chrome). This is a guild, not a careers
  portal.

## Strategic design principles

- **Quests + earnings are first-class.** It's a job board; money and work lead.
- **Product-bold, not theatrical:** amplify hierarchy, weight contrast, one sharp
  accent, committed density. Reserve drama for trust-free zones (hero, empty states).
- **User-owned data shows.** Verified identity, peer endorsements, and portable
  records are surfaced, not hidden.
- **Accessibility is non-negotiable:** WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-operable, every
  component state present.
